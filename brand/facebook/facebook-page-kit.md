## 2. Create the page – step by step

1. On Facebook (app or facebook.com), open **Menu → Pages → Create**.
2. **Page name:** `Leyu Games`. **Category:** `Video Game`. **Bio:** the bio above. Tap **Create Page**.
3. **Profile picture:** upload `facebook-profile-720.png`.
4. **Cover photo:** upload `facebook-cover-1640x856.png`. Check it on your phone too and drag it to centre if needed.
5. **Edit page info:** add the About text, website, email and username (Settings → Page setup → Username).
6. **Action button:** tap **Add button → Learn more**, link `https://t.me/LeyuGamesBot`.
7. **Restrict to adults:** Settings → Privacy → **Page and tagging / Age restrictions** → set **18+**.
   Optionally **Country restrictions** → show only in **Ethiopia**.
8. **Post the launch post** (section 4) with `facebook-launch-post-1080x1350.png`, then **Pin** it (⋯ → Pin to top).
9. Invite your friends to like the page, and share the page link in your Telegram channel and bot messages.

## 3. Important: Facebook's rules for real-money games

- **Ads and boosted posts:** Meta does **not** allow ads (including "Boost post") for real-money gambling, bingo
  or betting **without Meta's prior written permission**. Apply first with your licence:
  Meta Business Help Center → "Online gambling and games" → request authorisation. Without it, ads are
  rejected and the ad account can be blocked.
- **Normal posts** on your page are fine. Keep them truthful: no "guaranteed winnings", no "get rich", nothing
  aimed at people under 18, and always mention **18+** and **play responsibly**.
- Don't use other brands' names or logos (e.g. competitors' bingo names) in your posts.

## 5. Posting plan (simple)

- **3–4 posts a week**, same times (evenings, 7–9 PM, work well in Ethiopia).
- Mix: how-to (deposit, withdraw, play), promo codes with a clear end date, new rooms or games, reminders to
  play responsibly.
- Reply to comments and messages within a day; send payment questions to the bot or your support account.
- Reuse the same picture style (navy and gold, logo, @LeyuGamesBot) so people recognise the brand.

**Hashtags:** `#LeyuGames #ልዩጌምስ #Bingo #ቢንጎ #TelegramGames #Ethiopia` (3–6 per post is enough)

Have the Amharic texts checked by a native speaker before posting.
