## Boost settings

- **Goal:** "Get more website visits" (link clicks) to `https://t.me/LeyuGamesBot`.
- **Audience:** create your own:
  - **Location:** Ethiopia (or only the cities you want).
  - **Age:** **18–65+** (never below 18; Meta requires it for gambling ads).
  - **Interests (optional):** Games, Online games, Telegram, Mobile games, Entertainment.
- **Placements:** Facebook and Instagram feeds (square image), Stories and Reels (tall image).
- **Budget to start:** a small daily budget for 5–7 days, then keep the version with the cheaper clicks.
- **Track it:** compare the number of new players in your dashboard (Overview → new players) before and after.

## What keeps the ad from being rejected

Already done in the images and text above. Keep it this way when you edit:

- No promises of winnings or money ("win 10,000 birr", "get rich", "easy money").
- No mention of guaranteed or "free" money unless the offer is real and its rules are stated.
- Always show **18+** and **play responsibly**.
- No images of cash, children or famous people; no other brands' names or logos.
- The link must go to your own bot (t.me/LeyuGamesBot).

## Before you boost: get Meta's permission

1. In **Meta Business Suite**, make sure the page and ad account are in a **Business Portfolio** in your company's
   name.
2. Open **Meta Business Help Center** → search **"online gambling and gaming"** → fill in the **application form
   for online gambling ads**.
3. Attach your **gaming licence** and company details, list Ethiopia as the target country, and give
   facebook.com/leyugames and t.me/LeyuGamesBot.
4. Wait for written approval (usually a few working days to a few weeks). Then boost.
