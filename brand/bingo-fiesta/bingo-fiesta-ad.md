## Settings

- **Location:** Ethiopia. **Age:** 18+.
- **Goal:** link clicks to `https://t.me/BingoFiestaBot`.
- **Category question** (Meta may ask "Is this about a special category?"): answer truthfully. It is a free game;
  if asked about gambling, say entry is free with no purchase and link your prize rules.

## Must be true before you run it

The ad says "free", "no purchase" and "cash prizes", so all of these must be true in the bot:

1. **Entry is really free:** no deposit, no paid cards, no paid extra chances, nothing to buy.
2. **No link to Leyu Games or any real-money bot** (no buttons, mentions, messages or invites leading there). If
   players are moved from the free bot to a real-money one, platforms treat the ad as a gambling ad.
3. **Published prize rules** in the bot: what the prizes are, how winners are chosen, how and when prizes are paid,
   who can take part (18+, Ethiopia), and how to claim.
4. **Prizes are actually paid** as the rules say.
5. **Permit:** prize games of chance generally need approval from Ethiopia's **National Lottery Administration**,
   even when free to enter. Check with them (or a lawyer) before launching.

## Platform notes

- **Facebook / Instagram:** free-to-enter prize games are normally allowed as ordinary ads when the points above
  hold. Meta can still review prize promotions; keep the rules page ready.
- **TikTok:** stricter about anything gambling-like; a bingo ad may still be rejected in some countries. Try it
  with a small budget first.
- **Telegram Ads:** check promote.telegram.org/guidelines for games and contests before submitting.
